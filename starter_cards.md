# DFS Protocol — 20 Starter Cards

## Dual-Layer Syntax Structure
Each card operates on two layers:
- **Surface:** What the card appears to do in standard play
- **Deep:** What the card activates within the DFS Protocol framework

Rule 0 governs all card interactions without exception.

---

## Starter Card Set

### Card S01 — The Anchor
- **Surface:** Hold your current position; no player may move you this turn
- **Deep:** Declare intrinsic grounding; Fiction Die results do not apply to you this turn

### Card S02 — The Mirror
- **Surface:** Reflect any action played against you back to its source
- **Deep:** Expose the Banker mechanic behind the action; all players see the Deep layer

### Card S03 — The Reveal
- **Surface:** Look at any one hidden card currently in play
- **Deep:** Activate awareness protocol; the revealed card's Deep layer is read aloud

### Card S04 — The Pause
- **Surface:** Skip your turn voluntarily; gain two influence tokens
- **Deep:** Enter Selah state; all compounding effects on your position are locked for one turn

### Card S05 — The Compound
- **Surface:** Stack the effects of any two cards currently in your hand
- **Deep:** Both Deep layers activate simultaneously; effects compound rather than sequence

### Card S06 — The Inversion
- **Surface:** Swap the positions of any two players on the map
- **Deep:** Trigger Inversion protocol; both players' Fiction Die results are reset to zero

### Card S07 — The Broadcast
- **Surface:** Announce any one rule currently in play to all players
- **Deep:** Activate News Infusion; the announced rule is reinforced for two full turns

### Card S08 — The Freeze
- **Surface:** Prevent any one player from taking action this turn
- **Deep:** Suspend all Banker influence tokens held by that player for one turn

### Card S09 — The Restore
- **Surface:** Return any one card from the discard pile to active play
- **Deep:** Restored card re-enters with both Surface and Deep layers fully active

### Card S10 — The Sovereign Move
- **Surface:** Move to any unoccupied square on the map
- **Deep:** Declare sovereign territory on arrival; no Banker mechanic may enter that square this turn

### Card S11 — The Witness
- **Surface:** Observe all actions taken this turn without participating
- **Deep:** Log all Fiction Die results from this turn; they are available for review next turn

### Card S12 — The Override
- **Surface:** Cancel any one action currently in play
- **Deep:** Invoke Wizard of Iz review; the cancelled action is flagged for Rule 0 assessment

### Card S13 — The Frequency
- **Surface:** Set the tone for the next three turns; all players operate under your declared frequency
- **Deep:** Engage Magnar Humble Sovereign frequency; Banker archetypes lose one influence token per turn

### Card S14 — The Debt Marker
- **Surface:** Place a debt marker on any one square; players landing there lose one turn
- **Deep:** Flag the square as Fiction Die territory; Real Die governs all exits from that square

### Card S15 — The Citadel
- **Surface:** Protect your current square from all outside influence for one turn
- **Deep:** Establish protocol sovereignty over your position; no mechanic may displace you

### Card S16 — The MonipYouLie™
- **Surface:** Identify one manipulation mechanic currently operating on the board
- **Deep:** Name the Banker archetype behind it; all players confirm awareness before play continues

### Card S17 — The Decoder
- **Surface:** Read the hidden layer of any one card currently in play
- **Deep:** Full Deep layer is revealed to all players; Banker influence on that card is suspended

### Card S18 — The Selah Square
- **Surface:** Land on or activate a Selah Square; take one full turn of reflection
- **Deep:** All compounding effects pause; player re-enters next turn with full protocol awareness

### Card S19 — The Standing Order
- **Surface:** Remind all players of the current Standing Order
- **Deep:** All committed layers from this session lock and compound simultaneously

### Card S20 — The Rule 0 Card
- **Surface:** Invoke Rule 0 at any point during play
- **Deep:** All actions currently in play are reviewed; any that create a victim are immediately void
