# DFS Protocol — Rulebook Opening

## Rule 0: Create No Victims
The foundational principle of all DFS Protocol gameplay.  
No mechanic, card, or player action, could result in a victim being created.  
This rule supersedes all other rules, in every situation.

---

## The Banker
The Banker is the central antagonist archetype in DFS Protocol.  
The Banker operates through manipulation, misdirection, and manufactured scarcity.  
Recognizing Banker behavior is a core gameplay skill.

**Banker Visual Specifications:**
- Appears as a figure in formal attire
- Always positioned at a distance from direct consequences
- Uses language that obscures real costs

---

## The Die System

### Fiction Die (Negative Range: -1 to -6)
- Represents manufactured reality
- Outcomes are Banker-influenced
- Players must identify and counter Fiction Die results

### Real Die (Positive Range: 0 to 5)
- Represents grounded, intrinsic reality
- Outcomes are based on actual value
- Players build toward Real Die outcomes

---

## Wizard of Iz — Discretion Protocol
The Wizard of Iz serves as the human-in-the-loop arbiter.  
When a ruling is unclear, the Wizard of Iz applies Rule 0 as the deciding standard.  
No automated outcome overrides the Wizard of Iz when victims are at risk.
