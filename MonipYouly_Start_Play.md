## Welcome. You're in the right place.

### What Is This?
DFS Protocol is a strategy game built on one rule:

> **Rule 0: Create No Victims.**

Everything else flows from that.

---

**Step 2 — Pick Up Your Starter Cards**

→ (./Starter_Cards.md)

**Step 3 — Find Your Square on the Map**
The board has 24 squares. Safe Harbor squares are your starting zones.
→ (./Game_Map.md)

---

### The One Thing to Know Before You Play

There are two dice:
- **Fiction Die** (-1 to -6) — the Banker's world
- **Real Die** (0–5) — the Sovereign's world

Your job is to play the Real Die.
