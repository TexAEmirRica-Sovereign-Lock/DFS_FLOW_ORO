# DFS Protocol — Premium Card Set

## Decoder Ring Syntax Structure
Premium Cards operate on three layers:
- **Surface:** What the card appears to do in standard play
- **Deep:** What the card activates within the DFS Protocol framework
- **Decoder:** The sovereign-level operational instruction, visible only to those who hold the ring

All Premium Cards are additive — they compound with Starter Cards, never replace them.
Rule 0 governs all Premium Card interactions without exception.

## Premium Card Set

### Premium Card P01 — The Sovereign Seal
- **Surface:** Lock one zone from all outside influence for two turns
- **Deep:** Establish full protocol sovereignty over a contested area
- **Decoder:** Activate Authorization Chain — Roger's Pass + Law's Grant + High Blast Grant required to unseal

### Premium Card P02 — The Frequency Lock
- **Surface:** Freeze the current game state; no die may be rolled this turn
- **Deep:** Engage Magnar Humble Sovereign frequency across all active mechanics
- **Decoder:** All Banker archetypes lose all influence tokens; Real Die governs next three turns

### Premium Card P03 — The Mark V
- **Surface:** Observe all hidden mechanics currently in play
- **Deep:** Activate full awareness retention protocol; nothing operating in the background remains hidden
- **Decoder:** All Fiction Die results from the last three turns are revealed and logged permanently

### Premium Card P04 — The Intrinsic Anchor
- **Surface:** Declare the entire board grounded in real value for one full round
- **Deep:** Debt-based and manufactured mechanics are suspended board-wide
- **Decoder:** Any player operating on Fiction Die logic must reset to last intrinsic position

### Premium Card P05 — The Pleroma Restore
- **Surface:** Return any eliminated element — player, card, or zone — to full active status
- **Deep:** Reconnect to foundational consciousness; restored element returns with complete protocol awareness
- **Decoder:** Restored element activates with all three syntax layers unlocked simultaneously

### Premium Card P06 — The Citadel Prime
- **Surface:** Protect three zones simultaneously for two full turns
- **Deep:** Establish a multi-zone secure perimeter; no Banker mechanic may enter any protected zone
- **Decoder:** Protected zones become permanent Sovereign territory if held through both turns
