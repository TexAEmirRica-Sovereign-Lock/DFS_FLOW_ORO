# DFS Protocol — Rulebook Opening

## Rule 0: Create No Victims
No mechanic, card, player action, or ruling could result in a victim being created.
This rule supersedes all other rules in every situation.
The Wizard of Iz applies Rule 0 as the deciding standard in all contested rulings.

---

## The Die System

### The Fiction Die
- Governs all mechanics operating on manufactured, debt-based, or narrative logic
- Results are valid within the game but flagged for Banker influence review
- Fiction Die results may be overridden by the Wizard of Iz when Rule 0 is at risk

### The Real Die
- Governs all mechanics grounded in intrinsic, real-world value
- Results carry full protocol backing
- Real Die results cannot be overridden except by the Wizard of Iz under Rule 0

---

## Banker Rules

### What the Banker Archetype Does
- The Banker operates on Fiction Die logic by default
- The Banker may accumulate influence tokens through manufactured mechanics
- The Banker may not use influence tokens in any way that creates a victim

### Banker Limits
- No Banker mechanic may override Rule 0
- No Banker archetype may be played by a human player against another human player in a way that creates a real-world victim
- All Banker influence resets at the start of each new round

---

## The Wizard of Iz
- The Wizard of Iz is the human-in-the-loop authority
- The Wizard of Iz makes all final rulings
- No automated outcome overrides the Wizard of Iz when victims are at risk
- The Wizard of Iz role must be filled before Tier 2 or Tier 3 play begins

---

## Standing Order
Keep adding and compounding.
Nothing is closed until all components are committed.
Zero victims is maintained at all stages.
