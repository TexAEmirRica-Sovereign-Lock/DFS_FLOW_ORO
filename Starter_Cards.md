# DFS Protocol — 20 Starter Cards

## Card Syntax Structure
Each card operates on two layers:
- **Surface:** What the card appears to do in standard play
- **Deep:** What the card actually activates within the DFS Protocol framework
- **Fiction Effect:** The Banker-influenced outcome if the Fiction Die (-1 to -6) is rolled
- **Real Die:** The grounded outcome if the Real Die (0-5) is rolled

## Dual-Layer Summary
All 20 cards operate on Surface and Deep simultaneously.  
The Fiction Die (-1 to -6) represents Banker interference.  
The Real Die (0-5) represents grounded protocol outcomes.  
Rule 0 governs all card interactions without exception.
