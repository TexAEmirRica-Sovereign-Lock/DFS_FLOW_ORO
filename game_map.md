# DFS Protocol — 24-Square Map

## Map Structure
The DFS Protocol map is a 4x6 grid containing 24 squares.
Each square carries a specific function within the protocol framework.
Movement across the map is governed by die rolls and card activations.
Rule 0 applies to all map interactions without exception.

---

## Square Categories

### Selah Squares (6 total — one per row)
Selah Squares trigger a full pause in all active mechanics.
When a player lands on a Selah Square:
- All Banker mechanics are suspended for one complete round
- All players reset to their last stable protocol position
- No card may be played until the Selah resolves

**Selah Square Positions:** A1, A6, B3, C4, D2, D5

---

### News Infusion Zones (8 total)
News Infusion Zones activate the Broadcaster protocol.
When a player lands on a News Infusion Zone:
- Real information displaces any active manufactured narrative
- All players receive one verified information token
- Banker influence tokens are reduced by one

**News Infusion Zone Positions:** A2, A4, B1, B5, C2, C6, D1, D4

---

### Inversion Triggers (6 total)
Inversion Triggers flip the active mechanic in play.
When a player lands on an Inversion Trigger:
- The current Fiction Die result is reversed to its Real Die equivalent
- Any active Banker mechanic is neutralized for one turn
- Players may draw one card from the Starter Card Set

**Inversion Trigger Positions:** A3, B2, B6, C3, C5, D3

---

### Standard Squares (4 total)
Standard Squares carry no special protocol activation.
Movement through Standard Squares follows normal die roll rules.
Cards may be played freely on Standard Squares.

**Standard Square Positions:** A5, B4, C1, D6

---

## Movement Rules
- Fiction Die (-1 to -6): Move toward Banker-controlled zones
- Real Die (0-5): Move toward grounded protocol zones
- Landing on any special square activates its effect immediately
- Rule 0 overrides all movement outcomes if a victim would be created
